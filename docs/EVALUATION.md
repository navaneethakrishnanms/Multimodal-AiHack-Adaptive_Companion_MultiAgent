**How to read this.** F1 is computed on the gap set each policy would show the student. For C (and the ablation row `B_rootfilter`) the set is restricted to root causes, because RootCause deliberately hides downstream symptoms; the 'root-cause unknown' columns score every policy against exactly that target, the 'all unknown' columns against every hidden unknown topic. The ablation shows how much of C's gain comes from the filter alone. 'True gap closure' is the fraction of initially unknown topics the simulated student actually learned, which is the quantity personalization is meant to raise.

## Limitations (read before quoting any number)

- Simulated students encode our own assumptions (prerequisite structure, review success rate). Gains show the policy exploits prerequisite structure when it exists; they are not evidence about real learners.
- The courses are small (13 and 6 source units, 15 and 10 topics) and the held-out gate split has 28 queries.
- The first calibration of the refusal gate used a dev split without hard negatives and gave a poor held-out refusal rate; we added harder cases before re-running and did not move existing cases (history note in `tests/testset.json`).
- LLM-judged metrics appear only when an LLM was reachable during the run; otherwise they are marked *not measured*.
